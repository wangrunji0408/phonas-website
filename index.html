常见问题

FAQ

Phonas 支持哪些 NAS 设备？

Phonas 通过标准 SMB 协议连接，支持所有启用了 SMB 服务的设备，包括但不限于 Synology、QNAP、TrueNAS、OpenWrt、Windows 共享文件夹、macOS 共享等。

支持哪些照片格式？

当前支持 JPEG、HEIC/HIF、PNG、RAW（DNG、ARW）、MP4、MOV。

如何连接到我的 NAS？

在应用中添加服务器，输入 SMB 服务器地址、用户名和密码即可。确保您的设备与 NAS 在同一局域网内，或已配置远程访问。

免费试用后会怎样？

免费试用 3 天后，如果不购买，将只能访问本地相册。

What NAS devices does Phonas support?

Phonas connects via the standard SMB protocol and supports any device with SMB enabled, including Synology, QNAP, TrueNAS, OpenWrt, Windows shared folders, macOS sharing, and more.

What photo formats are supported?

Currently supported: JPEG, HEIC/HIF, PNG, RAW (DNG, ARW), MP4, MOV.

How do I connect to my NAS?

Add a server in the app and enter your SMB server address, username, and password. Make sure your device is on the same local network as your NAS, or that remote access is configured.

What happens after the free trial?

After the 3-day free trial, you will only be able to access the local photo library if you don't purchase.
